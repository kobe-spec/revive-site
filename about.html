Why Revive exists

Working with local businesses, I kept seeing the same thing. Owners spent real money and energy trying to get new customers in the door. Meanwhile, hundreds or thousands of people they had already served were sitting in their booking system, hearing almost nothing from them.

Those people already know the business. They’ve already paid. Many of them would happily come back, try something new or send a friend. Nobody was asking them.

It wasn’t because owners didn’t care. They were busy running the business. Figuring out what to send, who to send it to and when to follow up is a job on its own, and the texting button in their software doesn’t do any of it.

So Revive does that job. We manage SMS marketing to the customers a business has already earned, and we treat that customer list like what it is: one of the most valuable things the business owns.

Before spending more to get new customers, get more value from the ones you’ve already earned.
