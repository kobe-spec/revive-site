Fair questions

Is this just another marketing agency?+

No. We don’t run ads, build websites or chase strangers. Revive focuses on one thing most agencies ignore: the customers you’ve already earned. We market to them by text so they come back, buy more and send friends.

How do I know this will work for my business?+

Results depend on your list, your services and your offer, so we won’t promise a number. What we can say: people who already chose you once are far easier to bring back than strangers. On the call we’ll look at your customer base and tell you honestly whether it makes sense. You can also see what happened for Julie.

Do I have to switch booking software?+

No. Revive works with the customer list you already have. Keep running your business the way you do now.

Who is Revive a good fit for?+

Established local businesses with a few hundred customers or more and services people come back for: med spas, salons, massage and wellness studios, estheticians, chiropractors, pet groomers, auto detailers, fitness studios and similar.

Won’t texting my customers annoy them?+

Not when it’s done well. We send fewer, better messages to the right people, written in your voice. Every text includes an easy way to opt out, and we only message customers who have agreed to hear from you.

What happens on the 15-minute call?+

We’ll ask about your business and roughly how many customers you have, share a few campaign ideas that would fit, and answer your questions. If it isn’t a fit, we’ll say so.
