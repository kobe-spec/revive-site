Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

No mobile information will be sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent records are not shared with any third party, except the subcontracted service providers strictly required to deliver the messages (for example our messaging platform and telecom carriers).

Who we are

Revive Reputation ("we", "us") is a reputation management and local SEO service for private healthcare and wellness practices. This policy explains what we collect through revivereputation.com and through the review and messaging services we operate on behalf of our clients.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. Name, business name, email address and phone number when you contact us through our website chat or by email.
  • Client patient data. Contact details a client practice provides from its own patient or customer records, so that we can send review requests and appointment messages on that practice's behalf.
  • Service activity. Message delivery status, opt-out status, review submissions, and the performance metrics we report back to the practice.
  • Basic website data. Standard server and analytics information such as pages viewed, referring page, approximate region, browser and device type.

How we use it

We use this information only to deliver and support the service: responding to enquiries, sending the review requests and appointment messages a practice has engaged us to send, honouring opt-outs, reporting results to the practice, billing, and meeting our legal obligations. We do not use it for any unrelated purpose, and we do not sell it.

SMS and consent

Messages are sent only to recipients who have given prior express written consent — either directly to us, or to the practice we message on behalf of, at the point of booking, intake or sign-up. We do not message purchased, rented or scraped lists. Consent is never a condition of purchase or of receiving care. Full program details, message frequency and opt-out instructions are in our Terms of Service.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that make the service work — our messaging and CRM platform, telecom carriers, email provider, and hosting and analytics providers — and only to the extent needed to perform that function on our behalf. We also share a practice's own data back with that practice. We may disclose information where required by law. We never sell or rent personal information, and we never share mobile numbers or consent data for third-party marketing.

Retention and security

We keep information for as long as we provide the service to the relevant practice, plus the period needed for legal and record-keeping purposes (including consent and opt-out records). We use access controls and reputable vendors to protect it, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You can reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing kobe@revivereputation.com. Where a request concerns data held on behalf of a practice, we will work with that practice to fulfil it.

Children

Our service is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes and contact

If we update this policy we will change the date above and post the new version at this address. Questions, data requests or opt-out help: kobe@revivereputation.com.
