What did Revive actually do?

Julie already had customers. Instead of spending money trying to reach strangers, we marketed back to people who had already booked with her.

She runs her practice. We handled the marketing.

  • 01Planned the campaign around who to reach and what to offer
  • 02Wrote the messages in Julie’s voice, with her input
  • 03Sent them to the right past clients by text
  • 04Handled the follow-up so replies turned into booked appointments

The campaign behind the result

This wasn’t an ad campaign.

It went to customers Julie had already earned: a welcome-back text with 20% off, then a friendly follow-up for anyone who hadn’t replied. No paid ads.
